# Ps3p

Ps3p is a PlayStation 3 emulator for iPhone and iPad: the full RPCS3 core, running through
ARMSX3's iOS port, with its own animated menu.

## What Ps3p adds
- **Flow:** an animated background with light ribbons and drifting lights. It tilts with the device (parallax), and you can pick its colour or let it follow the time of day.
- **Category bar:** a floating glass bar at the top with a live clock. The selection slides between categories and the icons bounce.
- **Game library:** glass cards that animate as they scroll into view.
- **Intro:** an animated opening with its own startup sound.
- **Music and sounds:** looping menu music and sounds for navigating, confirming, going back, starting a game and earning a trophy. All of them are original and generated by `tools/make_audio.py`.
- A **Ps3p** section in Settings for colour, floating lights, music and sounds.

## Requirements
- iOS 17.4 or later, sideloaded with SideStore, AltStore or similar.
- JIT turned on, through StikDebug or the built-in JIT setup.
- Your own PS3 system firmware (PS3UPDAT.PUP), dumped from your console.

## Build
Every push to `main` runs **Ps3p iOS** on GitHub Actions. The unsigned `Ps3p.ipa` is uploaded
as a build artifact. The first run builds LLVM for iOS and caches it, so it takes a long time.
